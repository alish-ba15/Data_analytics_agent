### Recommendations  
1. **Focus on quantity‑boosting tactics** (e.g., bundle offers, volume discounts) because quantity is the stronger predictor of total revenue.  
2. **Re‑evaluate discount strategy:** Since `Discount Applied` is not correlated with higher spend, consider redesigning discount rules (e.g., tiered discounts based on spend thresholds) and monitor the effect over time.
